COMMON MEDICATIONS AND THEIR USES:

1. Paracetamol (Acetaminophen)
- Uses: Reduces fever and relieves mild to moderate pain
- Common brands: Tylenol, Panadol
- Dosage: 500mg every 4-6 hours, maximum 4000mg per day
- Side effects: Nausea, rash, liver damage with overdose

2. Ibuprofen
- Uses: Anti-inflammatory, reduces pain and fever
- Common brands: Advil, Motrin
- Dosage: 200-400mg every 6-8 hours
- Side effects: Stomach upset, kidney issues with long-term use

3. Amoxicillin
- Uses: Broad-spectrum antibiotic for bacterial infections
- Common brands: Amoxil, Trimox
- Dosage: 500mg every 8 hours for 7-10 days
- Side effects: Diarrhea, rash, allergic reactions

4. Omeprazole
- Uses: Treats acid reflux, GERD, and stomach ulcers
- Common brands: Prilosec, Losec
- Dosage: 20mg once daily before meals
- Side effects: Headache, nausea, vitamin B12 deficiency

5. Metformin
- Uses: Controls blood sugar in Type 2 diabetes
- Common brands: Glucophage, Fortamet
- Dosage: 500mg twice daily with meals
- Side effects: Diarrhea, nausea, metallic taste

6. Atorvastatin
- Uses: Lowers cholesterol levels
- Common brands: Lipitor
- Dosage: 10-20mg once daily
- Side effects: Muscle pain, liver enzyme elevation

7. Sertraline
- Uses: Treats depression, OCD, and anxiety
- Common brands: Zoloft
- Dosage: 50mg once daily
- Side effects: Insomnia, nausea, sexual dysfunction

8. Lisinopril
- Uses: Treats high blood pressure and heart failure
- Common brands: Prinivil, Zestril
- Dosage: 10-20mg once daily
- Side effects: Cough, dizziness, high potassium levels

9. Levothyroxine
- Uses: Treats hypothyroidism
- Common brands: Synthroid, Levoxyl
- Dosage: 25-100mcg once daily
- Side effects: Heart palpitations, weight loss

10. Albuterol
- Uses: Treats asthma and breathing issues
- Common brands: Ventolin, Proventil
- Dosage: 2 puffs every 4-6 hours
- Side effects: Tremors, increased heart rate

EMERGENCY MEDICATION GUIDELINES:

11. Epinephrine (Adrenaline)
- Uses: Severe allergic reactions, anaphylaxis
- Common brands: EpiPen
- Dosage: 0.3-0.5mg IM injection
- Side effects: Palpitations, anxiety, headache

12. Naloxone
- Uses: Reverses opioid overdose
- Common brands: Narcan
- Dosage: 0.4-2mg IV, IM or intranasal
- Side effects: Withdrawal symptoms, nausea

VACCINES AND PREVENTIVE MEDICINES:

13. Influenza Vaccine
- Uses: Prevents seasonal flu
- Common brands: Fluzone, Fluarix
- Dosage: Single dose annually
- Side effects: Sore arm, mild fever, fatigue

14. COVID-19 mRNA Vaccine
- Uses: Prevents COVID-19 infection
- Common brands: Pfizer-BioNTech, Moderna
- Dosage: 2 doses 3-4 weeks apart
- Side effects: Fever, fatigue, muscle aches

SAFETY GUIDELINES:

- Always consult a doctor before taking any medication
- Check for drug interactions
- Report any adverse reactions immediately
- Store medications in a cool, dry place
